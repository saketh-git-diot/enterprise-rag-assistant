TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

1. Business Travel
Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim eligible travel expenses.

2. Manager Approval
Business travel should be approved by the employee's manager before booking.

3. Accommodation
Employees should use company-approved accommodation options where available.

4. Expense Claims
Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days of completing the business trip.

5. Receipts
Employees must provide receipts for expenses where required by the expense management system.

6. Personal Expenses
Personal expenses are not reimbursable unless explicitly permitted by company policy.

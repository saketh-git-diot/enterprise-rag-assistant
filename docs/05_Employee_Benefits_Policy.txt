EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY

1. Health Benefits
Eligible full-time employees may participate in company-provided health benefit programs.

2. Learning and Development
Employees may participate in approved training and professional development programs.

3. Wellness
Eligible employees may claim wellness benefits according to the applicable reimbursement policy.

4. Eligibility
Benefit eligibility may depend on employment status, location, and the specific benefit program.

5. Questions
Employees should contact Human Resources for clarification regarding benefit eligibility.

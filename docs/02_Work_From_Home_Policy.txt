WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Eligibility
Eligible employees may work remotely according to their team's hybrid working arrangement.

2. Approval
Work-from-home requests require manager approval unless the employee's team has a predefined remote schedule.

3. Working Hours
Employees working remotely must remain available during their agreed working hours and attend required meetings.

4. Equipment
Company-provided equipment must be used according to IT security requirements.

5. Security
Employees must not share company confidential information with unauthorized persons while working remotely.

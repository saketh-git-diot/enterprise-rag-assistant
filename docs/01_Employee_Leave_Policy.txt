EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy defines leave entitlements and procedures for employees.

2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.
Employees joining during the year receive leave on a prorated basis.

3. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate may be required for sick leave exceeding two consecutive working days.

4. Work From Home
Employees may request work-from-home arrangements subject to team requirements and manager approval.
Work from home is not a substitute for approved leave.

5. Leave Approval
Planned leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance.
Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests.

6. Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year.
